# Flagellocanth

> [!info] Resumo
> Peixe raro, encontrado em **Cambion Drift (Deimos), Surface Hotspot during Fass / Vome cycle with Processed Fass Residue as bait, using Spari / Ebisu peixesing spear**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "These specimens were originally parasites, breeding inside the wyrm-guts of Fass and scattered across Deimos in the debris of his explosive demise. They are drawn to his light."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Flagellocanth

**Como conseguir Flagellocanth:** aparece em **Cambion Drift (Deimos), Surface Hotspot during Fass / Vome cycle with Processed Fass Residue as bait, using Spari / Ebisu peixesing spear**.
Obtido por **pesca com lança** nos open worlds; o tipo de peixe depende do bioma, da hora do dia e da isca.
