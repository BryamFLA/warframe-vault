# Khut-Khut Venom Sac

> [!info] Resumo
> Parte de peixe, encontrado em **Cortado de Khut-Khut peixes com Fisher Hai-Luk in Cetus (Terra). Catch Khut-Khut in Plains of Eidolon ponds during the Day. Also drops from Thumpers no Plains of Eidolon**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "The poison is used to cure leather and as a reagent to produce an embalming fluid."
