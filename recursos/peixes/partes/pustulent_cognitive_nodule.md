# Pustulent Cognitive Nodule

> [!info] Resumo
> Parte de peixe, encontrado em **Cortado de Aquapulmo e Cryptosuctus peixes com Daughter no Necralisk (Deimos). Catch Aquapulmo in Cambion Drift surface hotspots during Vome, e Cryptosuctus on the Cambion Drift surface during Fass. Also drops from Requiem Obelisks e Rolizor Infested Cysts no Cambion Drift**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "The Infestation provokes the growth of multiple, seemingly redundant brain-like clusters, whose function may be linked to hivemind activity."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Pustulent Cognitive Nodule

**Como conseguir Pustulent Cognitive Nodule:** aparece em **Cortado de Aquapulmo e Cryptosuctus peixes com Daughter no Necralisk (Deimos). Catch Aquapulmo in Cambion Drift surface hotspots during Vome, e Cryptosuctus on the Cambion Drift surface during Fass. Also drops from Requiem Obelisks e Rolizor Infested Cysts no Cambion Drift**.
Partes de peixe saem ao **cortar (desmontar) peixes** pescados, no vendedor de pesca do hub (Fisher Hai-Luk em Cetus, The Business em Fortuna, Daughter no Necralisk).
