# Mortus Horn

> [!info] Resumo
> Parte de peixe, encontrado em **Cortado de Mortus Lungfish com Fisher Hai-Luk in Cetus (Terra). Catch Mortus Lungfish in Plains of Eidolon ponds at any time of day. Also drops from Thumpers no Plains of Eidolon**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "The horn of the Mortus Lungfish is ground down into a powder and used by the Ostrons in herbal remedies."
