# Saturated Muscle Mass

> [!info] Resumo
> Parte de peixe, encontrado em **Cortado de Ostimyr e Kymaeros peixes com Daughter no Necralisk (Deimos). Catch Ostimyr in Cambion Drift surface hotspots during Fass, e Kymaeros on the Cambion Drift surface during Fass e Vome. Also drops from Infested Cysts no Cambion Drift**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Environmental contamination is co-responsible for mutations, alongside Infested cellular alteration."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Saturated Muscle Mass

**Como conseguir Saturated Muscle Mass:** aparece em **Cortado de Ostimyr e Kymaeros peixes com Daughter no Necralisk (Deimos). Catch Ostimyr in Cambion Drift surface hotspots during Fass, e Kymaeros on the Cambion Drift surface during Fass e Vome. Also drops from Infested Cysts no Cambion Drift**.
Partes de peixe saem ao **cortar (desmontar) peixes** pescados, no vendedor de pesca do hub (Fisher Hai-Luk em Cetus, The Business em Fortuna, Daughter no Necralisk).
