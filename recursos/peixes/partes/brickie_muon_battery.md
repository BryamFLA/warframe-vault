# Brickie Muon Battery

> [!info] Resumo
> Parte de peixe, encontrado em **Dismantled from Brickie servofish com The Business in Fortuna (Vênus). Catch Brickie in Orb Vallis ponds e rivers during Warm weather. Also drops from resource containers across the Orb Vallis**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "A small, atomic power source."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Brickie Muon Battery

**Como conseguir Brickie Muon Battery:** aparece em **Dismantled from Brickie servofish com The Business in Fortuna (Vênus). Catch Brickie in Orb Vallis ponds e rivers during Warm weather. Also drops from resource containers across the Orb Vallis**.
Partes de peixe saem ao **cortar (desmontar) peixes** pescados, no vendedor de pesca do hub (Fisher Hai-Luk em Cetus, The Business em Fortuna, Daughter no Necralisk).
