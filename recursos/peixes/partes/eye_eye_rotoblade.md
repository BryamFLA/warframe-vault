# Eye-Eye Rotoblade

> [!info] Resumo
> Parte de peixe, encontrado em **Dismantled from Eye-Eye servofish com The Business in Fortuna (Vênus). Catch Eye-Eye in Orb Vallis ponds during Warm weather. Also drops from resource containers across the Orb Vallis**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "A machine element used to propel robotic organisms through coolant."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Eye-Eye Rotoblade

**Como conseguir Eye-Eye Rotoblade:** aparece em **Dismantled from Eye-Eye servofish com The Business in Fortuna (Vênus). Catch Eye-Eye in Orb Vallis ponds during Warm weather. Also drops from resource containers across the Orb Vallis**.
Partes de peixe saem ao **cortar (desmontar) peixes** pescados, no vendedor de pesca do hub (Fisher Hai-Luk em Cetus, The Business em Fortuna, Daughter no Necralisk).
