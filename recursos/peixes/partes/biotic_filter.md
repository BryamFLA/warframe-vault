# Biotic Filter

> [!info] Resumo
> Parte de peixe, encontrado em **Cortado de Aquapulmo e Duroid peixes com Daughter no Necralisk (Deimos). Catch Aquapulmo in Cambion Drift surface hotspots during Vome, e Duroid in Cambion Drift cave hotspots during Fass e Vome. Also drops from Requiem Obelisks e Auroron Entrati Caskets no Cambion Drift**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Sifting the glutinous Exocrine is an essential part of the survival process, and a reminder of the creature's original function."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Biotic Filter

**Como conseguir Biotic Filter:** aparece em **Cortado de Aquapulmo e Duroid peixes com Daughter no Necralisk (Deimos). Catch Aquapulmo in Cambion Drift surface hotspots during Vome, e Duroid in Cambion Drift cave hotspots during Fass e Vome. Also drops from Requiem Obelisks e Auroron Entrati Caskets no Cambion Drift**.
Partes de peixe saem ao **cortar (desmontar) peixes** pescados, no vendedor de pesca do hub (Fisher Hai-Luk em Cetus, The Business em Fortuna, Daughter no Necralisk).
