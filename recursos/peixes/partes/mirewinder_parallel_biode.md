# Mirewinder Parallel Biode

> [!info] Resumo
> Parte de peixe, encontrado em **Dismantled from Mirewinder servofish com The Business in Fortuna (Vênus). Catch Mirewinder in Orb Vallis caves during any weather. Also drops from resource containers across the Orb Vallis**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Controls the electrical flow throughout the circuitry within robotic organisms."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Mirewinder Parallel Biode

**Como conseguir Mirewinder Parallel Biode:** aparece em **Dismantled from Mirewinder servofish com The Business in Fortuna (Vênus). Catch Mirewinder in Orb Vallis caves during any weather. Also drops from resource containers across the Orb Vallis**.
Partes de peixe saem ao **cortar (desmontar) peixes** pescados, no vendedor de pesca do hub (Fisher Hai-Luk em Cetus, The Business em Fortuna, Daughter no Necralisk).
