# Spinal Core Section

> [!info] Resumo
> Parte de peixe, encontrado em **Cortado de Vitreospina e Chondricord peixes com Daughter no Necralisk (Deimos). Catch Vitreospina in Cambion Drift caves during Fass e Vome, e Chondricord in Cambion Drift cave hotspots during Fass e Vome. Also drops from Isolation Vault Bounties e Sagiant Entrati Caskets no Cambion Drift**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Many delicate and complex nerve fibers are housed in the jointed vertebrae, which are one of the creature's most resilient parts. Found from Vitreospina fish known to eke out a primitive existence in cave systems."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Spinal Core Section

**Como conseguir Spinal Core Section:** aparece em **Cortado de Vitreospina e Chondricord peixes com Daughter no Necralisk (Deimos). Catch Vitreospina in Cambion Drift caves during Fass e Vome, e Chondricord in Cambion Drift cave hotspots during Fass e Vome. Also drops from Isolation Vault Bounties e Sagiant Entrati Caskets no Cambion Drift**.
Partes de peixe saem ao **cortar (desmontar) peixes** pescados, no vendedor de pesca do hub (Fisher Hai-Luk em Cetus, The Business em Fortuna, Daughter no Necralisk).
