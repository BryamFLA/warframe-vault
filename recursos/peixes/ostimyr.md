# Ostimyr

> [!info] Resumo
> Peixe incomum, encontrado em **Cambion Drift (Deimos), Surface Hotspot during Fass cycle with Fass Residue as bait, using Spari / Ebisu peixesing spear**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Crammed with redundant, mutated internal organs, this pungent fish mingles Orokin and Infested ancestry. It is confined to the Exocrine and is only active by the light of the Wyrm Fass."

**Como conseguir Ostimyr:** aparece em **Cambion Drift (Deimos), Surface Hotspot during Fass cycle with Fass Residue as bait, using Spari / Ebisu peixesing spear**.
Obtido por **pesca com lança** nos open worlds; o tipo de peixe depende do bioma, da hora do dia e da isca.
