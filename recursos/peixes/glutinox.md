# Glutinox

> [!info] Resumo
> Peixe incomum, encontrado em **Cambion Drift (Deimos), Surface during Vome cycle with Fass Residue as bait, using Spari / Ebisu peixesing spear**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "These hefty fish are cannibalistic. They rise by Vome's light to absorb rare gases, retreating when Fass ascends to devour their smaller kin in their lairs."

**Como conseguir Glutinox:** aparece em **Cambion Drift (Deimos), Surface during Vome cycle with Fass Residue as bait, using Spari / Ebisu peixesing spear**.
