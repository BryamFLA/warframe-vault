## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Sapcaddy Venedo Case


**Da seção de aquisição da wiki:** Venedo Case is acquired by dismantling Sapcaddies caught through Fishing. Go to The Business in Fortuna, select the desired amount of Sapcaddy available, and select the "Dismantle" option to extract the components. Each Sapcaddy yields one Venedo Case. This resource also drops from containers in Orb Vallis.
