# Sentient Core

> [!note] O arquivo usa outro nome para este item
> O arquivo é `exceptional_sentient_core.md`, mas o nome do item no jogo é **Sentient Core**. O conteúdo abaixo é o do item real.

> [!info] Resumo
> Sentient Cores are Sentient objects that can be traded in for Standing with The Quills in Cetus. They are also used to craft Revenant and his Phantasma, and Verdilac. Sentient Cores are dropped from various Sentient enemies. They appear as white and blue orbs that float above the ground, lit up by a blue aura that makes them more visible, and marked with HUD icons much like Ayatan Stars.
>
> *Texto de flavor no jogo (inglês):* “An integral part of Vomvalysts and Teralysts, the exact function of this... device? Organ? is uncertain.”

### O que a página diz

On the Plains of Eidolon, Intact Sentient Cores are dropped by Eidolon Vomvalysts while Exceptional Sentient Cores are dropped by destroying the Synovia joints of the Eidolons. A Flawless Sentient Core is dropped upon successfully capturing (not defeating) an Eidolon through having them fully tethered by allied Eidolon Lures; both the Eidolon Shards and the Flawless Sentient Cores will be dropped by the Lures upon self-destruction, not by the Eidolon themself.
