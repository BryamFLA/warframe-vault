## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Orokin Ducats


**Da seção de aquisição da wiki:** Ducats can be acquired by selling Prime blueprints, Prime weapon parts, and crafted Prime Warframe components at the Void Trader's Kiosks found in the Concourse area in Relays (except Maroo's Bazaar). These Prime parts and blueprints, in turn, are acquired from Void Relics, or from trading with other players (note that Ducats themselves cannot be traded directly). Note that while accessing Baro Ki'Teer is time limited, the Void Trader's Kiosks can be accessed at any time. Ducats may also be purchased for Aya from Varzia during Prime Resurgence. Void Surplus have a 2.00% chance of containing 1,000 Ducats. Baro Ki'Teer sells one Void Surplus per visit for 50,000 Credits 50,000. The Ducats value depends on the item's rarity as it appears on the Void Relics that they originate from.
