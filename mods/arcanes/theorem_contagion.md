# Theorem Contagion

> [!info] Resumo
> Arcane de **Warframe**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Standing in a zone created by a Residual Arcane creates a globe that orbits the player every 2s. The globes will strike the nearest enemy within 15m dealing 150 damage increasing their vulnerability to the globe's damage type by 200% for 6s. Globes will persist for 30s upon leaving the zone.
