# Bursting Mass

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Mutalist Quanta**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** The infested mass accumulates 200% of the damage that goes through it and deals it in 15m radius when it expires. Secondary fire will manually detonate an existing mass.
