# Critical Mutation

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Catabolyst**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Each kill increases Critical Chance and Critical Damage by 30% up to 300%. Reduce by 30% when fewer than 3 enemies are struck by the grenade explosion.
