# Landslide

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Landslide Fists**, raridade **comum**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Stance: Bash enemies with an explosive sliding punch, repeat up to three times for a combo attack. Each hit in the three-punch combo increases the radius of the attack. Energy cost is reduced with each hit of Landslide. Install mods on the Landslide Fists to boost stats.
