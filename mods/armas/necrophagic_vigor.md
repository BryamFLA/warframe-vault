# Necrophagic Vigor

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Hema**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Reloading drains up to half your Health based on ammo created, increasing Critical Chance and Critical Damage by +20% for every 20 Health drained. Capped at 360%.
