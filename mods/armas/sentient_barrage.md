# Sentient Barrage

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Battacor**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Discharge from Alt-Fire now has infinite Body Punch Through but no longer explodes on contact. Each enemy hit will refill one charge, and reaching a full charge will increase the Critical Chance and Critical Damage of the next discharge by 300%.
