# Fatal Attraction

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Djinn**, raridade **comum**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Enemies within 20m are drawn to you. Once they are within 6m, they detonate for 250 Damage. Kill them before detonation to increase lure time and reduce cooldown.
