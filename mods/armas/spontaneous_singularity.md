# Spontaneous Singularity

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Simulor**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Orbs that strike targets will explode immediately with 90% Bonus Damage and a 18% chance of Singularity that pulls in more targets. Orbs do not bounce or combine.
