# Sentient Surge

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Ocucor**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Each target eliminated refills 20% of Ocucor's magazine. Status Chance and Critical Chance are increased by 60% for each Tendril active.
