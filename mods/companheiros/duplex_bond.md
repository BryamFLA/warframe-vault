# Duplex Bond

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Companion**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Companion will clone itself each time you expend 100 energy, up to 3 clones. Clones live 30s and cannot use Abilities. Their kills have a 50% chance of dropping Energy Orbs.
