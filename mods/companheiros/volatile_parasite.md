# Volatile Parasite

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Predasite**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Every 15s spits out a maggot that latches onto the nearest enemy, drawing fire from those within 40m. On death, the maggot bursts, dealing 100% of accumulated damage <DT_VIRAL_COLOR>Viral Damage (+40% damage if the enemy dies while latched) to those within 10m.
