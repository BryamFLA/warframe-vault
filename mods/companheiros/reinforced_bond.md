# Reinforced Bond

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Companion**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** If the Companion exceeds 1200 Max Shields or Overshields then your fire rate is increased 60%. Reloading restores 150 Overshields to your companion.
