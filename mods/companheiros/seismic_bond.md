# Seismic Bond

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Companion**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** While a channeled Ability is active, Companion melee attacks create a 4m shockwave for 30% of their melee attack damage. Damage dealt by your Companion increases your Ability Efficiency by 3% for 12 seconds. Max 10 stacks.
