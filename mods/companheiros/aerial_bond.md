# Aerial Bond

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Companion**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Airborne kills decrease Companion Recovery Time by 3s and 9s for headshot kills. Companion creates a field of cold that increases up to 35% and 10m radius while Warframe is airborne, lasting for 3s after returning to ground.
