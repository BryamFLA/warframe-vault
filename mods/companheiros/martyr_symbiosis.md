# Martyr Symbiosis

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Vulpaphyla**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Drains and stores 20 health from corpses within 25m up to 40% of your health. If your health falls below 10%, Companion downs itself to heal you.
