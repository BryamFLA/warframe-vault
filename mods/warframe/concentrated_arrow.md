# Concentrated Arrow

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Ivara**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Artemis Bow Augment: Fires a single arrow with +25% Base Critical Chance on full charge, additional +50% Chance and explodes in a 7m radius on Weak Points. Removes Punch Through.
