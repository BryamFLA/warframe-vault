# Molecular Fission

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Nova**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Molecular Prime Augment: Enemies hit by Null Stars are primed. When killed, primed enemies will restore a Null Star charge and have a 100% chance of restoring two.
