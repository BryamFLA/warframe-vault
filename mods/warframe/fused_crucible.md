# Fused Crucible

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Qorvex**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Crucible Blast Augment: Becomes a channeled ability consuming 20 <ENERGY> Energy/s that ramps up. While active Chyrinka Pillars trigger chain reactions and their duration is frozen. Mobility is greatly reduced.
