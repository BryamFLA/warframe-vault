# Insatiable

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Nidus**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Ravenous Augment: Nidus has a chance for additional Mutation stacks while in the infestation. The base 60% chance increases with power strength, and multiple stacks are possible above 100%.
