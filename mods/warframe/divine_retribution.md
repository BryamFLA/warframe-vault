# Divine Retribution

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Nezha**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Divine Spears Augment: Status Effects spread to all speared enemies. Spear explosions scale by 1.5x of remaining <DT_SLASH_COLOR>Slash, <DT_POISON_COLOR>Toxin and <DT_FIRE_COLOR>Heat Status Effects. Base Radius is 14m.
