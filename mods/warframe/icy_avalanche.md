# Icy Avalanche

> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Frost**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Avalanche Augment: Allies within Affinity Range are coated in ice that grants 60 Overguard per enemy hit. Overguard increases by 20% of your Armor once per enemy.
