> [!info] Resumo
> Mod de **Saryn**, raridade **raro**.
> **Efeito (rank máximo, texto do jogo):** Toxic Lash Augment: Create 5m toxic clouds, dealing 300 <DT_POISON_COLOR>Toxin Damage/s for 12s with every kill. Damage is twice as strong for Melee kills.
