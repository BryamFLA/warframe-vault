# Lato Vandal

> [!info] Resumo
> Arma **secundária** (Pistol), **MR 7**, dano principal **Corte**, introduzida no Update 7.0.

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Lato Vandal

Como conseguir a arma Lato Vandal:

Recompensa do **Elite Sanctuary Onslaught** (originalmente brinde da beta fechada, 2013). Peças negociáveis; exige MR 7 para o blueprint.

| Peça | Fonte (drop) | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Blueprint | Elite Sanctuary Onslaught (Elite Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. B/C | 3,16% |
| Blueprint | Elite Sanctuary Onslaught (Elite Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. A | 2,51% |
| Barrel | Elite Sanctuary Onslaught (Elite Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. B/C | 3,16% |
| Barrel | Elite Sanctuary Onslaught (Elite Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. A | 2,51% |
| Receiver | Elite Sanctuary Onslaught (Elite Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. B/C | 3,16% |
| Receiver | Elite Sanctuary Onslaught (Elite Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. A | 2,51% |

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Lato Vandal (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint** de Lato Vandal (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os componentes:** Barrel, Receiver.
3. **Junte os recursos e forje na Foundry:** 25.000 créditos + Barrel, Receiver. Tempo: **12 h** (pular: 50 platina).
4. **Upe a arma até o rank 30** para ganhar Mastery.

| Item | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lato Vandal** | 25.000 | — + Barrel, Receiver | 12 h |
