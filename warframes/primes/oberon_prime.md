# Oberon Prime

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe Prime de **suporte**, lançada no **Update 20.6.2**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Protect the balance with this regal forest guardian. Featuring altered mod polarities for greater customization."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Oberon Prime

Como conseguir a Warframe Oberon Prime:

| Peça Prime | Relíquias ativas (raridade) | Ativas / vaultadas |
|---|---|---|
| Blueprint | — | 0 / 7 |
| Neuroptics Blueprint | — | 0 / 5 |
| Chassis Blueprint | — | 0 / 7 |
| Systems Blueprint | — | 0 / 6 |

> Relíquias **vaultadas** não caem mais em missões; só voltam no **Prime Resurgence** (Varzia) ou por troca entre jogadores.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Oberon Prime (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Oberon Prime (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Prime Neuroptics, Prime Chassis, Prime Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 25.000 créditos + Prime Neuroptics, Prime Chassis, Prime Systems + 5 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prime Neuroptics | 15.000 | 10 [[recursos/raridades/raro/control_module.md\|Control Module]] + 4.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/alloy_plate.md\|Alloy Plate]] + 1.500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/circuits.md\|Circuits]] + 2 [[recursos/raridades/raro/nitain_extract.md\|Nitain Extract]] | 12 h |
| Prime Chassis | 15.000 | 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/gallium.md\|Gallium]] + 100 [[recursos/raridades/incomum/oxium.md\|Oxium]] + 1.250 [[recursos/raridades/comum/rubedo.md\|Rubedo]] + 7.500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/salvage.md\|Salvage]] | 12 h |
| Prime Systems | 15.000 | 2 [[recursos/raridades/raro/argon_crystal.md\|Argon Crystal]] + 250 [[recursos/raridades/incomum/plastids.md\|Plastids]] + 6.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/nano_spores.md\|Nano Spores]] + 1.250 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] | 12 h |
| **Oberon Prime** (montagem) | 25.000 | Prime Neuroptics, Prime Chassis, Prime Systems + 5 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 3 dia(s) |

## 🔮 Habilidades — Oberon Prime

**Passiva (inglês):** Oberon grants Righteous Negation to himself and his allies in <AFFINITY_SHARE>Affinity Range when he collects <HEALTH>Health Orbs, protecting players against the next instance of damage. Stacks up to 3 times.

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Smite** | 25 | Focuses deadly energy within a target, dealing massive damage and removing all of its defenses, including Overguard. Enemies near the target also take a portion of the damage. |
| 2 | **Hallowed Ground** | 50 | Sanctifies the ground around Oberon, inflicting <DT_RADIATION_COLOR>Radiation Damage to all enemies within the radius. Also grants protection against Status Effects for Oberon and his allies. |
| 3 | **Renewal** | 25 | Generates a protective aura that grants bonus Armor to Oberon and his allies and restores allies' Health over time. Healing is doubled while within the radius of Hallowed Ground. |
| 4 | **Reckoning** | 100 | Lifts enemies into the air and then hurls them down with conviction, removing their Armor. Enemies hit by this ability will grant Oberon additional Armor and have a chance to spawn a <HEALTH>Health Orb when they die. |
