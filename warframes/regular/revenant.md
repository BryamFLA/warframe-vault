# Revenant

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **dano, sobrevivência**, lançada no **Update 23.5**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Macabre spectral power has risen. Infused with the essence of the Eidolon, Revenant has strong survivability and deals high damage. Enemies become his thralls."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Revenant

Como conseguir a Warframe Revenant:

Blueprint principal ao concluir a quest **Mask of the Revenant** (liberada no Rank 2 dos **Quills**). Componentes: **bounties das Plains of Eidolon** — Systems nível 20–40, Chassis 30–50, Neuroptics 40–60; é preciso forjar 2 componentes para avançar a quest. Extras no Cephalon Simaris (50.000).

| Neuroptics | Cetus Bounty Lvl 40-60 Stage 4 — rot. A/B/C | 6,76% |

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Revenant (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Revenant (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 25.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 1 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md|Orokin Cell]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/neural_sensors.md\|Neural Sensors]] + 15 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/intact_sentient_core.md\|Intact Sentient Core]] + 6.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] + 2.200 [[recursos/raridades/comum/rubedo.md\|Rubedo]] | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/morphics.md\|Morphics]] + 10 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/intact_sentient_core.md\|Intact Sentient Core]] + 1.500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/rubedo.md\|Rubedo]] + 50 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/iradite.md\|Iradite]] | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/control_module.md\|Control Module]] + 3 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/cetus_wisp.md\|Cetus Wisp]] + 8.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/salvage.md\|Salvage]] + 3.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] | 12 h |
| **Revenant** (montagem) | 25.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 1 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 3 dia(s) |

## 🔮 Habilidades — Revenant

**Passiva (inglês):** Shield depletion smashes enemies within 7.5m with a 100 Damage knockdown shockwave.

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Enthrall** | 25 | Convert a target into a zealous thrall. Thralls turn on their allies and enthrall through damage. On death, they disintegrate into a damaging pillar of energy. The thrall horde remains under Revenants spell until this ability runs out. |
| 2 | **Mesmer Skin** | 50 | Become enveloped in Sentient energy, redirecting damage and stunning all those who dare attack. Stunned enemies can be Enthralled at no energy cost. |
| 3 | **Reave** | 50 | Dash through enemies as a wall of sentient energy, leeching shields and health from any encountered, enhanced for thralls. |
| 4 | **Danse Macabre** | 25 | Erupt with a multitude of Eidolon energy beams and sweep a circle of death around Revenant. The beams will modify their Damage Type to target select defenses, while incoming damage is redirected back into the beams. Hold fire to boost Status Effects and Damage, at the cost of increased energy consumption. Thralls killed by this ability leave overshield pickups. |

**Helminth:** a habilidade **Reave** pode ser subsumida (doada ao Helminth).

**Augments** vendidos por: [[mecanicas/sindicatos/cephalon_suda.md|Cephalon Suda]], [[mecanicas/sindicatos/perrin_sequence.md|The Perrin Sequence]].
