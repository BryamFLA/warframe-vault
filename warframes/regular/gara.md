# Gara

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **dano, sobrevivência, controle de grupo**, lançada no **Update 22**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Enemies are made fragile by Gara’s deadly touch. Gara’s vitric abilities provide her and her allies with survivability and deal high damage. Shatter the foe."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Gara

Como conseguir a Warframe Gara:

Blueprint principal ao concluir a quest **Saya's Vigil** (Cetus). Componentes: **bounties das Plains of Eidolon** (Cetus) — Chassis nível 5–15, Systems 10–30, Neuroptics 20–40. Blueprints extras: **Cephalon Simaris**, 50.000 de Standing.

| Peça | Fonte (drop) | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | Cetus Bounty Lvl 20-40 Final Stage — rot. C | 26,09% |
| Neuroptics | Cetus Bounty Lvl 20-40 Final Stage — rot. A | 24,62% |
| Neuroptics | Cetus Bounty Lvl 20-40 Final Stage — rot. B | 23,53% |
| Neuroptics | Cetus Bounty Lvl 20-40 Stages 2 & 3 — rot. C | 10,4% |
| Chassis | Cetus Bounty Lvl 5-15 Final Stage — rot. A/B/C | 30,56% |
| Chassis | Cetus Bounty Lvl 5-15 Stage 2 — rot. A/B/C | 7,52% |
| Systems | Cetus Bounty Lvl 10-30 Final Stage — rot. A/B/C | 25% |
| Systems | Cetus Bounty Lvl 10-30 Stage 2 — rot. A/B/C | 10,08% |

**Platina:** pronta no Mercado por **225 platina**.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Gara (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Gara (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 25.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md|Orokin Cell]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 2 [[recursos/raridades/raro/neurodes.md\|Neurodes]] + 65 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/grokdrul.md\|Grokdrul]] + 90 Pyrotic Alloy + 700 [[recursos/raridades/comum/circuits.md\|Circuits]] | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 2 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/cetus_wisp.md\|Cetus Wisp]] + 120 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/coprite.md\|Coprite Alloy]] + 55 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/iradite.md\|Iradite]] + 800 [[recursos/raridades/comum/rubedo.md\|Rubedo]] | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 2 [[recursos/raridades/raro/morphics.md\|Morphics]] + 35 Charc Electroplax + 25 Esher Devar + 850 [[recursos/raridades/comum/circuits.md\|Circuits]] | 12 h |
| **Gara** (montagem) | 25.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 3 dia(s) |

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shattered Lash** | 25 | Lash out with stream of shattered glass, or hold for an arcing strike. Stats are boosted by the equipped mods on Shattered Lash. |
| 2 | **Splinter Storm** | 50 | Gara's armor splinters into a maelstrom of shattered glass. Allies who contact the cloud are fortified against damage. |
| 3 | **Spectrorage** | 75 | Trap enemies in a carousel of mirrors, forcing them to attack visions of their true selves. Destroyed mirrors damage their attackers, as does the collapse of the carousel. |
| 4 | **Mass Vitrify** | 75 | Create an expanding ring of molten glass that slowly crystallizes enemies who enter. When the expansion is complete, the ring hardens to block weapons fire.
