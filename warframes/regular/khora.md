# Khora

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **controle de grupo, dano**, lançada no **Update 22.18.0**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Khora is red in whip and claw. She works together with her Kavat companion, Venari, to deal high damage and provide crowd control. Two bodies, one will."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Khora

Como conseguir a Warframe Khora:

Blueprint principal no **Mercado**. Componentes: **Sanctuary Onslaught** normal (não Elite); cada 2 estágios é uma rotação (A-A-B-C).

| Peça | Fonte (drop) | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | Normal Sanctuary Onslaught (Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. C | 9,09% |
| Neuroptics | Normal Sanctuary Onslaught (Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. A/B | 7,14% |
| Chassis | Normal Sanctuary Onslaught (Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. C | 9,09% |
| Chassis | Normal Sanctuary Onslaught (Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. A/B | 7,14% |
| Systems | Normal Sanctuary Onslaught (Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. C | 9,09% |
| Systems | Normal Sanctuary Onslaught (Sanctuary Onslaught, Sanctuary Onslaught) — rot. A/B | 7,14% |

**Platina:** pronta no Mercado por **325 platina**.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Khora (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Khora (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 25.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md|Orokin Cell]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 2 Kavat Genetic Code + 35 Kuaka Spinal Claw + 1.500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] + 16.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/salvage.md\|Salvage]] | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 5 Kavat Genetic Code + 65 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/iradite.md\|Iradite]] + 12.250 [[recursos/raridades/comum/alloy_plate.md\|Alloy Plate]] + 4.300 [[recursos/raridades/comum/rubedo.md\|Rubedo]] | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 2 Kavat Genetic Code + 1.000 [[recursos/raridades/incomum/plastids.md\|Plastids]] + 2.600 [[recursos/raridades/comum/circuits.md\|Circuits]] + 11.500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/ferrite.md\|Ferrite]] | 12 h |
| **Khora** (montagem) | 25.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 3 dia(s) |

## 🔮 Habilidades — Khora

**Passiva (inglês):** The ferocious kavat, Venari, fights by Khora's side and provides her with a 15% speed boost while active. If killed, Venari will reappear after 45s.

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Whipclaw** | 25 | Send enemies reeling with a deafening whipcrack. Stats are boosted by the equipped mods on Whipclaw. |
| 2 | **Ensnare** | 50 | Bind a hapless target in living metal, entangling others who stray too close. Whipclaw will refresh the trap allowing it to capture more enemies. |
| 3 | **Venari** | 0 | Command Venari to focus on a target. Hold to cycle between Attack, Protect, and Heal postures. If Venari is killed, use this ability to revive her instantly. |
| 4 | **Strangledome** | 100 | Weave a dome of living chain that ensnares and strangles any enemy within, and any foolish enough to approach. Foes outside the trap will try to hasten their comrade's deaths by shooting them. Crack Whipclaw on the dome to further damage any trapped enemies. |

**Helminth:** a habilidade **Ensnare** pode ser subsumida (doada ao Helminth).

**Augments** vendidos por: [[mecanicas/sindicatos/red_veil.md|Red Veil]], [[mecanicas/sindicatos/steel_meridian.md|Steel Meridian]].
