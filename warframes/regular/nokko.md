# Nokko

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **dano, controle de grupo**, lançada no **Update 40**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Spring into action with Nokko! With his strong roots and an array of tricky fungi this young defender will quickly grow on you"

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Nokko

Blueprints nas **bounties do Deepmines** (Fortuna, drop incomum/raro; exige The New War). Alternativa: comprar do **Nightcap** com **Fergolyte** (160 por componente, 240 pelo principal, 720 no total), no Rank 4.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Nokko (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Nokko (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 25.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md|Orokin Cell]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 50 Tepa Nodule + 10 [[recursos/open_worlds/orbe_vallis/echowinder_shell.md\|Echowinder Anoscopic Sensor]] + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/neurodes.md\|Neurodes]] + 500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/nano_spores.md\|Nano Spores]] | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 30 [[recursos/open_worlds/orbe_vallis/thermal_sludge.md\|Thermal Sludge]] + 500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/alloy_plate.md\|Alloy Plate]] + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/gallium.md\|Gallium]] + 700 [[recursos/raridades/comum/nano_spores.md\|Nano Spores]] | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 40 [[recursos/open_worlds/cambion_drift/mytocardin.md\|Mytocardia Spore]] + 20 [[recursos/open_worlds/orbe_vallis/gorgaricus_spore.md\|Gorgaricus Spore]] + 250 [[recursos/raridades/comum/circuits.md\|Circuits]] + 2 [[recursos/raridades/raro/argon_crystal.md\|Argon Crystal]] | 12 h |
| **Nokko** (montagem) | 25.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 3 dia(s) |

## 🔮 Habilidades — Nokko

**Passiva (inglês):** Upon receiving fatal damage, Nokko reverts to his Sprodling form and his active mushrooms will glow, reviving Nokko when touched. Orbs spawned will grant a speed boost but not healing. If a glowing mushroom is not reached in time, Nokko will automatically use a self-revive.

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stinkbrain** | 25 | Throw a mushroom that periodically sheds poisonous spores, inflicting <DT_VIRAL_COLOR> Viral Damage and Status Effect upon nearby enemies while also lulling them to sleep. |
| 2 | **Brightbonnet** | 50 | Release a rejuvenating mushroom that emits a radial pulse, giving Nokko and his allies' <ENERGY> Energy and applying a buff to Ability Strength that lasts for a short time. |
| 3 | **Reroot** | 50 | Revert to Sprodling form, spawning orbs that heal Nokko and grant a speed boost. While in this state, Nokko is untargetable and heals gradually. |
| 4 | **Sporespring** | 75 | Unleash a chaotic ballistic mushroom that seeks enemies and bounces explosively off whatever it touches. Contact with any of Nokko's mushrooms invigorates them, doubling their pulse rate. |

**Helminth:** a habilidade **Brightbonnet** pode ser subsumida (doada ao Helminth).

**Augments** vendidos por: [[mecanicas/sindicatos/red_veil.md|Red Veil]], [[mecanicas/sindicatos/perrin_sequence.md|The Perrin Sequence]].
