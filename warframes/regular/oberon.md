# Oberon

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **suporte**, lançada no **Update 11.5**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Healing and suffering do Oberon’s bidding. His command of the natural world supports allies. Boon to his friends, bane of his foes."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Oberon

Como conseguir a Warframe Oberon:

Blueprint principal no **Mercado**. Componentes: **Points of Interest** de Railjack — Neuroptics e Systems na **Earth Proxima**, Chassis na **Saturn Proxima**.

| Peça | Fonte (drop) | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | Earth Proxima Point of Interest — rot. A | 10% |
| Chassis | Saturn Proxima Point of Interest — rot. A | 10% |
| Systems | Earth Proxima Point of Interest — rot. A | 10% |

**Platina:** pronta no Mercado por **325 platina**.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Oberon (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Oberon (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 25.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 1 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md|Orokin Cell]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 150 [[recursos/raridades/comum/alloy_plate.md\|Alloy Plate]] + 1 [[recursos/raridades/raro/control_module.md\|Control Module]] + 150 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] + 500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/circuits.md\|Circuits]] | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 1 [[recursos/raridades/raro/gallium.md\|Gallium]] + 1.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] + 300 [[recursos/raridades/comum/rubedo.md\|Rubedo]] | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 1 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] + 1 [[recursos/raridades/raro/gallium.md\|Gallium]] + 500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] + 220 [[recursos/raridades/comum/alloy_plate.md\|Alloy Plate]] | 12 h |
| **Oberon** (montagem) | 25.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 1 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 3 dia(s) |

## 🔮 Habilidades — Oberon

**Passiva (inglês):** Oberon grants Righteous Negation to himself and his allies in <AFFINITY_SHARE>Affinity Range when he collects <HEALTH>Health Orbs, protecting players against the next instance of damage. Stacks up to 3 times.

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Smite** | 25 | Focuses deadly energy within a target, dealing massive damage and removing all of its defenses, including Overguard. Enemies near the target also take a portion of the damage. |
| 2 | **Hallowed Ground** | 50 | Sanctifies the ground around Oberon, inflicting <DT_RADIATION_COLOR>Radiation Damage to all enemies within the radius. Also grants protection against Status Effects for Oberon and his allies. |
| 3 | **Renewal** | 25 | Generates a protective aura that grants bonus Armor to Oberon and his allies and restores allies' Health over time. Healing is doubled while within the radius of Hallowed Ground. |
| 4 | **Reckoning** | 100 | Lifts enemies into the air and then hurls them down with conviction, removing their Armor. Enemies hit by this ability will grant Oberon additional Armor and have a chance to spawn a <HEALTH>Health Orb when they die. |

**Helminth:** a habilidade **Smite** pode ser subsumida (doada ao Helminth).

**Augments** vendidos por: [[mecanicas/sindicatos/new_loka.md|New Loka]], [[mecanicas/sindicatos/steel_meridian.md|Steel Meridian]].
