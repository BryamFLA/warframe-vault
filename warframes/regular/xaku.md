# Xaku

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **dano**, lançada no **Update 29**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Broken Warframes adrift in the Void converged to create Xaku. Together, they deal high damage. Xaku proves that the sum of the whole is greater than its parts."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Xaku

Como conseguir a Warframe Xaku:

Blueprint principal ao concluir a quest **Heart of Deimos**. Componentes: **bounties do Cambion Drift** (Deimos) — Neuroptics nível 15–25, Systems 30–40, Chassis 40–60 ou Steel Path; a partir do 2º estágio.

| Peça | Fonte (drop) | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | Necralisk Bounty Lvl 15-25 Final Stage — rot. A/B/C | 12% |
| Neuroptics | Necralisk Bounty Lvl 15-25 Stage 2 — rot. A/B/C | 8,82% |
| Chassis | Necralisk Bounty Lvl 40-60 Final Stage — rot. A/B/C | 13,04% |
| Chassis | Steel Path Necralisk Bounty Lvl 100-100 Final Stage — rot. A/B/C | 13,04% |
| Chassis | Necralisk Bounty Lvl 40-60 Stages 2 & 3 — rot. A/B/C | 8,57% |
| Chassis | Steel Path Necralisk Bounty Lvl 100-100 Stages 2 & 3 — rot. A/B/C | 8,57% |
| Systems | Necralisk Bounty Lvl 30-40 Final Stage — rot. A/B | 11,32% |
| Systems | Necralisk Bounty Lvl 30-40 Stage 2 & 3 — rot. A/B | 8,22% |
| Systems | Necralisk Bounty Lvl 30-40 Stage 4 — rot. A/B | 6% |

**Platina:** pronta no Mercado por **325 platina**.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Xaku (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Xaku (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 25.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 10 [[recursos/raridades/raro/neural_sensors.md\|Neural Sensors]] + 60 Thaumic Distillate + 20 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/sharrac_tegmen.md\|Sharrac Teeth]] + 40 Esher Devar | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 5 [[recursos/open_worlds/orbe_vallis/gyromag_systems.md\|Gyromag Systems]] + 3 Spinal Core Section + 50 [[recursos/open_worlds/orbe_vallis/venerdo.md\|Venerdo Alloy]] + 50 [[recursos/open_worlds/cambion_drift/lucent_terrogel.md\|Lucent Teroglobe]] | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 10 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/breath_of_the_eidolon.md\|Breath Of The Eidolon]] + 5 Scintillant + 45 [[recursos/open_worlds/cambion_drift/pustulite.md\|Pustulite]] + 60 [[recursos/open_worlds/cambion_drift/ganglion.md\|Ganglion]] | 12 h |
| **Xaku** (montagem) | 25.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 3 dia(s) |
