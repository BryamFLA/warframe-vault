# Kullervo

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **dano**, lançada no **Update 33.5**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Kullervo answers for his sins and the sins of others. His daggers and melee prowess deal high damage. Seize the power of treachery to slake his thirst for revenge."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Kullervo

Como conseguir a Warframe Kullervo:

Blueprints comprados da **Acrithis** (Dormizone, Duviri) com **Kullervo's Bane**: 15 pelo principal e 9 por componente (42 no total). Banes vêm de derrotar o **Kullervo** na ilha **Kullervo's Hold** e concluir a Spiral (Orowyrm) no Duviri — 4 a 6 por run (6 a 8 no Steel Path). Recomendado: **The Duviri Experience**.

**Platina:** pronta no Mercado por **325 platina**.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Kullervo (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Kullervo (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 30.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 10 [[recursos/open_worlds/duviri/pathos_clamp.md|Pathos Clamp]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 50 Lamentus + 50 Tasoma Extract + 100 Saggen Pearl + 100 Connla Sprout | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 125 Nacreous Pebble + 100 Kovnik + 75 Rune Marrow + 100 Ueymag | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 100 Aggristone + 100 Eevani + 360 Ariette Scale + 100 Ueymag | 12 h |
| **Kullervo** (montagem) | 30.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 10 [[recursos/open_worlds/duviri/pathos_clamp.md\|Pathos Clamp]] | 3 dia(s) |

## 🔮 Habilidades — Kullervo

**Passiva (inglês):** Kullervo boasts +75% Heavy Attack Efficiency and +100% Heavy Attack Wind Up Speed on all melee weapons.

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wrathful Advance** | 25 | (TAP) Kullervo charges a Heavy Attack, then teleports to his target and strikes. His rage-filled focus temporarily increases his Melee Critical Chance.r (HOLD) Teleport to any marked location. |
| 2 | **Recompense** | 50 | Kullervo surrounds himself with daggers. Each dagger that strikes an enemy restores his health, but each dagger that misses an enemy strikes Kullervo, dealing a bit of damage. When Kullervo has max Health, he receives Overguard from struck enemies. |
| 3 | **Collective Curse** | 75 | Kullervo sends forth a curse that binds enemies. When a cursed enemy takes damage from him, every other cursed enemy also suffers a portion of that damage. |
| 4 | **Storm of Ukko** | 100 | The Void answers Kullervo's invocation with a storm of daggers that rain down upon his enemies. |
