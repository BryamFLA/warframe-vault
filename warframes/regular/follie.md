# Follie

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **controle de grupo**, lançada no **Update 42**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Follie, the merry, macabre Shadowgrapher, brings ink to life. Support her squad and control enemy crowds with a myriad of versatile sketches and tricks."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Follie

Como conseguir a Warframe Follie:

Blueprints na **rotação A** do **Follie's Hunt** em **Vesper Relay, Vênus** (exige Chains of Harrow). Alternativa: comprar da **Aspirant Zorba** (qualquer Relay) com **Atramentum** — 400 por componente e 1.200 pelo principal.

| Peça | Fonte (drop) | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Blueprint | Follie's Hunt (Steel Path) — rot. A | 6% |
| Blueprint | Follie's Hunt (Vesper Relay, Vênus) — rot. A | 5% |
| Neuroptics | Follie's Hunt (Steel Path) — rot. A | 6% |
| Neuroptics | Follie's Hunt (Vesper Relay, Vênus) — rot. A | 5% |
| Chassis | Follie's Hunt (Steel Path) — rot. A | 6% |
| Chassis | Follie's Hunt (Vesper Relay, Vênus) — rot. A | 5% |
| Systems | Follie's Hunt (Steel Path) — rot. A | 6% |
| Systems | Follie's Hunt (Vesper Relay, Vênus) — rot. A | 5% |

**Platina:** pronta no Mercado por **370 platina**.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Follie (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Follie (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 30.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 2 [[recursos/raridades/raro/neurodes.md\|Neurodes]] + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/gallium.md\|Gallium]] + 150 [[recursos/raridades/raro/cryotic.md\|Cryotic]] + 2 [[recursos/raridades/raro/neural_sensors.md\|Neural Sensors]] | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 800 [[recursos/raridades/comum/alloy_plate.md\|Alloy Plate]] + 2 [[recursos/raridades/raro/argon_crystal.md\|Argon Crystal]] + 800 [[recursos/raridades/comum/ferrite.md\|Ferrite]] + 600 [[recursos/raridades/comum/salvage.md\|Salvage]] | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 250 [[recursos/raridades/comum/circuits.md\|Circuits]] + 500 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] + 4 [[recursos/raridades/raro/morphics.md\|Morphics]] + 50 [[recursos/raridades/incomum/oxium.md\|Oxium]] | 12 h |
| **Follie** (montagem) | 30.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 3 dia(s) |

## 🔮 Habilidades — Follie

**Passiva (inglês):** INKBLOT: Follie's abilities coat enemies in ink that slows their movement by 50% for 10s. Afflicted enemies have a 20% chance to drop Health and Energy Orbs.

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Forced Perspective** | 15 | Drop through an inky frame and emerge invulnerable at the aimed location in a splash of ink that inflicts Inkblot on nearby enemies. |
| 2 | **Shadowgraph** | 25 | Bring shadowgraphs from Follie's sketchbook to life as items that can be used for tactical advantage. Shadowgraphs splash Inkblot onto nearby enemies when created.r r HOLD to open the Shadowgraph gear wheel. |
| 3 | **Self Portrait** | 50 | Draw an ink effigy to absorb the damage dealt to Follie and allies. Below, a pool of ink spreads Inkblot. Kill enemies inside the pool to grow Follie's effigy and its ink puddle. |
| 4 | **Plein Air** | 75 | Tie nearby enemies to floating balloons that douse them with Inkblot. Pop the balloons to send them crashing to the ground, splashing damage on nearby enemies. |

**Helminth:** a habilidade **Self Portrait** pode ser subsumida (doada ao Helminth).
