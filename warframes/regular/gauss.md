# Gauss

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **dano, sobrevivência**, lançada no **Update 25.7**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Take charge with the Saint of Altra. Gauss is fast, deals high damage, and has strong survivability. Movement charges his powerful battery, so never stop moving."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Gauss

Como conseguir a Warframe Gauss:

Blueprint principal no **Mercado**. Componentes: **rotação C do Disruption** em **Kappa, Sedna** (7,84% cada).

| Peça | Fonte (drop) | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | Sedna Disruption (Kappa, Sedna) — rot. C | 7,84% |
| Chassis | Sedna Disruption (Kappa, Sedna) — rot. C | 7,84% |
| Systems | Sedna Disruption (Kappa, Sedna) — rot. C | 7,84% |

**Platina:** pronta no Mercado por **325 platina**.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Gauss (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Gauss (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 25.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md|Orokin Cell]]. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 1 [[recursos/raridades/raro/argon_crystal.md\|Argon Crystal]] + 1.600 [[recursos/raridades/comum/rubedo.md\|Rubedo]] + 6.200 [[recursos/raridades/comum/salvage.md\|Salvage]] + 2.950 [[recursos/raridades/comum/alloy_plate.md\|Alloy Plate]] | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 3 [[recursos/railjack/radian_sentirum.md\|Radian Sentirum]] + 3 Heart Nyth + 6 Star Crimzian + 55 [[recursos/open_worlds/eidolon/grokdrul.md\|Grokdrul]] | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 3 Radiant Zodian + 3 [[recursos/open_worlds/orbe_vallis/marquise_thyst.md\|Marquise Thyst]] + 70 [[recursos/open_worlds/cambion_drift/mytocardin.md\|Mytocardia Spore]] + 85 [[recursos/open_worlds/orbe_vallis/thermal_sludge.md\|Thermal Sludge]] | 12 h |
| **Gauss** (montagem) | 25.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 3 dia(s) |

## 🔮 Habilidades — Gauss

**Passiva (inglês):** Moving generates an electrical current that fills Gauss' battery. Shields recharge up to 120% faster while the Recharge Delay is up to 80% shorter, based on the battery level.

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mach Rush** | 15 | Burst into a hyper-sprint bowling over enemies and charging the battery. Crashing into solid objects generates a powerful shockwave. Hold to rush continuously. |
| 2 | **Kinetic Plating** | 50 | Generate armor plating that converts a portion of absorbed Kinetic Damage (Physical, <DT_FIRE_COLOR>Heat, <DT_FREEZE_COLOR>Cold, and <DT_EXPLOSION_COLOR>Blast) into Energy. Also protects Gauss from being staggered or knocked down. Damage Resistance is relative to the battery level. |
| 3 | **Thermal Sunder** | 50 | Siphon kinetic energy from the area, charging the battery and inflicting <DT_FREEZE_COLOR>Cold Status on nearby enemies. Hold reverses the process, draining the battery and inflicting <DT_FIRE_COLOR>Heat Status on nearby enemies. |
| 4 | **Redline** | 100 | Push Gauss' battery beyond the redline, supercharging his Abilities and setting Fire Rate, Attack Speed, Reload Speed, and Casting Speed into overdrive. When past the redline, bolts of arcing electricity dance periodically from Gauss, exploding en masse when the ability is deactivated. |

**Helminth:** a habilidade **Thermal Sunder** pode ser subsumida (doada ao Helminth).

**Augments** vendidos por: [[mecanicas/sindicatos/arbiters_of_hexis.md|Arbiters of Hexis]], [[mecanicas/sindicatos/perrin_sequence.md|The Perrin Sequence]].
