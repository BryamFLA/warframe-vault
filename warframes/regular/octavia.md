# Octavia

> [!info] Resumo
> Warframe de **suporte, dano, controle de grupo**, lançada no **Update 20**.
> *Descrição no jogo (inglês):* "Octavia turns bass, beat, and melody into a symphony of devastation. The music of her Mandachord gives her high survivability and supports allies. Her composition means the enemy’s destruction."

## 🌍 Onde Conseguir — Octavia

Como conseguir a Warframe Octavia:

Blueprint principal e Mandachord da quest **Octavia's Anthem**. **Chassis**: puzzle musical na **Lua**. **Neuroptics**: rotação C de **Terrorem** (Survival, Deimos). **Systems**: cache da rotação A de **Plato** (Lua). Extras no Cephalon Simaris.

| Peça | Fonte (drop) | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | Orokin Derelict Survival (Terrorem, Deimos) — rot. C | 22,56% |
| Systems | Lua Exterminate Resource Caches — rot. A | 22,56% |

**Platina:** pronta no Mercado por **225 platina**.

## 🏗️ Como Construir — Octavia (passo a passo)

1. **Consiga o blueprint principal** de Octavia (seção anterior).
2. **Consiga os blueprints dos componentes:** Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems.
3. **Forje cada componente** na Foundry (tabela abaixo).
4. **Forje a Warframe:** 25.000 créditos + Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 1 Mandachord. Tempo: **3 dia(s)** (pular: 50 platina).

| Componente | Créditos | Recursos | Tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroptics | 15.000 | 1.000 [[recursos/raridades/incomum/oxium.md\|Oxium]] + 4.800 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] + 2.600 [[recursos/raridades/incomum/plastids.md\|Plastids]] + 5 [[recursos/raridades/raro/neural_sensors.md\|Neural Sensors]] | 12 h |
| Chassis | 15.000 | 6.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/polymer_bundle.md\|Polymer Bundle]] + 25.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/salvage.md\|Salvage]] + 1.500 [[recursos/raridades/incomum/plastids.md\|Plastids]] + 3 [[recursos/raridades/raro/neurodes.md\|Neurodes]] | 12 h |
| Systems | 15.000 | 2.000 [[recursos/raridades/raro/kuva.md\|Kuva]] + 3.000 [[recursos/raridades/comum/circuits.md\|Circuits]] + 20 [[recursos/raridades/raro/morphics.md\|Morphics]] + 5 [[recursos/raridades/raro/orokin_cell.md\|Orokin Cell]] | 12 h |
| **Octavia** (montagem) | 25.000 | Neuroptics, Chassis, Systems + 1 Mandachord | 3 dia(s) |

## 🔮 Habilidades — Octavia

**Passiva (inglês):** Replenish 30 energy over 30s for Octavia and allies within 15m when abilities are activated.

| # | Habilidade | Energia | Descrição (inglês) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mallet** | 25 | Rhythmically beats damage into nearby enemies and draws their fire. Damage inflicted on the Mallet increases its lethality. |
| 2 | **Resonator** | 50 | Launches a rollerball that charms foes to follow it. Combines with the Mallet to create a roving ball of sonic destruction. |
| 3 | **Metronome** | 75 | Grants buffs to those who consistently perform actions in time to Octavia's music. Timed jumps offer the Vivace speed buff. Crouching on the beat grants cloaking with the Nocturne buff. Firing rhythmically bestows Opera multishot buff. Timed melee swings give the Forte damage buff. |
| 4 | **Amp** | 100 | Draws power from the decibel level of sound in the area and uses it to amplify a damage buff for Octavia and her allies. It also doubles the damage and range of nearby Mallets. |

**Helminth:** a habilidade **Resonator** pode ser subsumida (doada ao Helminth).

**Augments** vendidos por: [[mecanicas/sindicatos/cephalon_suda.md|Cephalon Suda]], [[mecanicas/sindicatos/new_loka.md|New Loka]].
